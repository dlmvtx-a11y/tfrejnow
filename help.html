🚫 Blocking Ads (the single biggest comfort upgrade)

The video servers are free third-party embeds, and some of them run ads or popups to pay for themselves - that's outside this site's control. The fix isn't on our end, it's a browser extension on yours. Takes 2 minutes, works for the rest of your browsing too.

💻 On a computer

  • Best option: switch to Firefox and install uBlock Origin

    Free, open-source, and the strongest ad/popup blocker available - Chrome restricted what extensions are allowed to do a while back, so Firefox is genuinely the more powerful option now, not just a preference.

  • Staying on Chrome or Edge?

    Install uBlock Origin Lite from the Chrome Web Store (switch its mode to "Complete" in the extension's settings after installing), or AdGuard. Both still block most ads and popups, just not quite as thoroughly as the Firefox version.

  • Prefer not to install anything?

    The Brave browser blocks ads and popups by default with zero setup.

📱 On mobile

  • Android

    Install Firefox for Android, then add uBlock Origin from within it - same full-strength blocking as desktop. Alternatively, the AdGuard app blocks ads system-wide (in-browser and inside other apps too), with a free tier.

  • iPhone / iPad

    Apple limits what browser extensions can do on iOS. Install AdGuard for Safari from the App Store as a Safari content blocker, or set up a private DNS filter (see below) for broader coverage.

🌐 One setting that covers every device on your WiFi

Set your phone, computer, or router's DNS to a free filtering service like NextDNS or AdGuard DNS. This blocks known ad/tracker domains network-wide - including apps and smart TVs - without installing anything per-device. Takes about 5 minutes to set up once.

🎬 Servers

Every title has 5 servers to choose from, shown as tabs above the player. If one doesn't load, is slow, or looks wrong, just tap a different one - they're independent, so this is completely normal and happens with every free streaming source. Server 1 also supports auto-next-episode and remembers your exact spot more accurately than the others.

Found a server that's consistently broken for a specific title? Tap ⚠️ Report Broken next to the server list to let the admin know.

👤 Profiles

Up to 5 profiles per account, each with their own watchlist, continue watching, and history - completely separate from each other, like Netflix.

  • PIN - optional, protects a specific profile from being opened by someone else on the account.
  • Kids mode - restricts that profile to family-friendly titles only.
  • Hide Genres - available on any profile, not just Kids - hide entire genres you're not interested in seeing.
  • Switch profiles any time from the account menu (top right) without signing out.

🎉 Watch Together

Start a private watch party from any title page and share the link - friends can join with just a name, no account needed.

🔗 Watch Room

Everyone plays the video independently on their own device from the same starting point. Most reliable option - use this if you just want to hang out and chat while watching roughly together.

📡 Live Stream (genuinely synced)

The host shares their screen live, and everyone watches that exact feed in real time - true frame-by-frame sync. When starting the share, be sure to pick "This Tab" specifically (not "Entire Screen"), and confirm it's showing the right tab if you have multiple open. Quality depends on the host's upload speed, and the host needs to keep that tab open for the whole watch.

🎤 Voice Chat

Tap "Join Voice" in either mode to talk live with everyone in the party. Mute anytime with the button that appears next to it.

Host controls

As host, you pick the server/episode for everyone, and can remove anyone from the party (tap the "watching" count to see who's in, with a Kick option next to each name).

⚠️ If a connection won't complete (stuck on "waiting" or "connecting"), the most common cause is an ad-blocker or privacy extension blocking WebRTC on that device - the page will tell you if this looks like what's happening. Temporarily disabling it for this site usually fixes it.

📌 Everything Else

  • 🔔 Notifications - the bell icon tells you when a show in your Continue Watching or Watchlist has a new episode, or when a Watchlist movie is finally out.
  • 👍 Like / Dislike - on any title page. Liking things (and what you actually watch) shapes your "Recommended For You" row on the homepage.
  • Search & Filters - each section (Movies, TV, Anime) has its own search and genre filters, scoped to just that type.
  • 📱 Install as an app - on mobile, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option for a faster, full-screen experience without opening a browser each time.
  • 📊 My Stats - see your total watched, top genre, and more from your Profile page.
  • 📋 Updates - what's changed on the site recently, from the account menu.
